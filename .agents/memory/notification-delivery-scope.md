---
name: Notification delivery scope
description: The allowed delivery channels for notifications in this catering inventory project.
---

Notifications in this app are in-app inbox records only. Do not send email, SMS, push notifications, or other outbound alerts to users.

**Why:** The user explicitly set this boundary; no further reason was specified.

**How to apply:** Keep operational notifications inside the authenticated app inbox. Do not add external notification channels or scheduled outbound alert delivery.
